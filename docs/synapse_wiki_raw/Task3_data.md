In BraTS-GoAT, we will use **preoperative** MR data collected for the previous BraTS challenges; specifically:

* RSNA-ASNR-MICCAI BraTS Continuous Evaluation Challenge (Segmentation - Adult Glioma)
* BraTS Challenge on Sub-Sahara-Africa Adult Glioma (Segmentation - BraTS-Africa)
* ASNR-MICCAI BraTS Intracranial Meningioma Challenge (Segmentation - Meningioma)
* ASNR-MICCAI BraTS Brain Metastasis Challenge (Segmentation - Brain Metastases)
* ASNR-MICCAI BraTS Pediatrics Tumor Challenge (Segmentation - Pediatric Tumors)

> ####! ATTENTION: participants will  **ONLY** be allowed to use the data provided through the BraTS-GoAT sub-challenge website _(link coming soon)_.
> ####! The use of any additional data, either private or publicly available (including previous BraTS challenges), will result in the announcement of immediate disqualification from current and future BraTS challenges in the challenge forum.
 
####! **Training set:**
- Full BraTS Adult gliomas training data + ground truth.
- BraTS Meningiomas training data: ground truth will be available only for a subset of the data.
- BraTS Brain metastases training data: ground truth will be available only for a subset of the data.
 
####! **Validation set:**
- BraTS Adult gliomas validation data.
- BraTS Africa validation data.
- BraTS Meningiomas validation data.
- BraTS Brain Metastases validation data.
- BraTS Pediatric Tumors validation data.

####! **Test set:**
The composition of the private test data will **NOT** be disclosed to the participants.

---

###! Data Access

In addition to registering for the challenge, you must also request access to the data:

1. **Submit the form:** complete the [Data Access Google form](https://forms.gle/UiCpXos2zKFPdMnK6). Only one access form is needed across all 5 challenge tasks and their training + validation datasets\*.

2. **Accept your invite:** once your details are verified, the BraTS Service Account will email you an invitation to join the @BraTS2026DataAccessTeam . Accept it to unlock the files below!

<small> \* *Note: test datasets and validation GT labels will not be released to the public.*</small>
